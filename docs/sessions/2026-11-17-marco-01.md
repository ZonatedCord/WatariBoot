## F-44 (colours): mapping identified from the photos
A model reproduces the photos: Windows writes BGRX8 pixels with X = 0xFF, and the panel reads each 32-bit word as `x2r10g10b10` [verified: simulation against the video frames; still to be confirmed by D5 on the panel].
- R10 = (R >> 4) | (X & 0x3F) << 4, so red sits near maximum on every pixel.
- G10 = (G >> 2) | (R & 0xF) << 6.
- B10 = B | (G & 3) << 8.

Black therefore shows as pure red. The red "stop screen" is probably Windows' black stop screen [hypothesis: depends on the WinPE build]. The blue logo shows as magenta, orange and yellow stripes, because the low bits of G and R jump the neighbouring 10-bit channel.

No GOP format lines up with a 10-bit packed panel, and the truthful bit-mask GOP gave a black screen on the bench (F-36). **Decision (Marco):** a shadow buffer converted by the layer.

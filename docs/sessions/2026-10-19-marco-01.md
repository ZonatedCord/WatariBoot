## 2026-10-20/21: M2, Windows 11 on top of Watari EL2 (verified by Opus)
- **Windows 11 ARM64 reaches the lock screen on top of Watari EL2** in QEMU TCG, 4 cores, in both cpuif modes (`hw` and `trap`), about 3-5 minutes per boot. The installed VM disk is used with `snapshot=on`; EDK2 finds Windows through the NVMe fallback boot option. Launcher: `el2/boot/run-m2.sh`. Screenshot: `docs/img/m2-lockscreen-trap.png`.
- Obstacles (F-23): PSCI conduit is SMC for Windows (FADT), not HVC; with `its=off` Windows hangs in an NVMe INTx storm, also without the layer (control run), so the bench keeps the ITS and the vGIC passes LPIs through; RAM maps extended to 5 GB; `CPU_ON` entry range was too small (bugcheck 0xA0 with 4 cores only).
- **#43 answered:** in trap mode Windows writes `ICC_PMR_EL1` 9 times in a whole boot and 0 times per second at idle. The real trap-mode load is `ICC_IAR1/EOIR1` (~1800 pairs/s idle on 4 cores): input for #52 on the M1.
- Verification by Opus: `make -C el2 test` and `el2/boot/run-all.sh` 15/15 re-run; an independent trap-mode boot (lock screen in ~3 min, 9 PMR writes); an independent code review found two LPI queue bugs (duplicate entries, 16-bit LR owner) and two risks, fixed with tests and confirmed by another lock-screen boot.
- New issues: #62 (device interrupts on the M1 without an ITS), #63 (soak test), #64 (LPI hardening), #65 (INTx storm, side track).

# Session 2026-11-16 (Ciani, with Marco): M3a Monday on the real M1

**Goal:** run `docs/procedures/m3a-monday-runbook.md`: day 3 (EDK2 on the layer, the gate) and day 4 (Windows Setup from a RAM disk) on Marco's M1 (j293, 8 GB), Ciani's Mac as host, results read from photos and video of the M1 screen.

## Result
- **Day 3 gate PASS:** E (ehw2) PASS, D0 PASS, D1 PASS (13/0/0, R/G/B/Y bars), D2 and D3 PASS (EDK2 text 0.5 s after the jump, self-test HOLD ~15 s after it), D4 PASS (`trap-notc`, eight cores, no `CPU_ON` from EDK2). `re/m3a-day3-edk2.md`.
- **PM0: NOLOG.** The M1's DRAM does not keep the post-mortem ring across a ~10 s power-off.
- **Day 4:** W1a PASS (write 11.38 MB/s, read 27.98 MB/s: the 713 MiB image in ~1 min). **W2 and W1c FAIL at stage S1:** the RAM disk is registered and BDS starts `BOOTAA64.EFI` from it, then a synchronous exception (`ESR 0x02000000`) stops it in EDK2's handler (`ASSERT [ARMCPUDXE] CPUEXCEPTIONHANDLERLIB.C(438)`), with both `trap-notc`/8 cores and `trap`/1 core; layer `LAST ERROR: NONE`. No fallback (not a core-count symptom). F-39; `re/m3b-winram-day4.md`.
- **Tooling:** three false FAILs in `monday.py` checks (F-40), all verified by hand.
- **Incident:** five forced power-offs without a macOS boot; the M1 came up in the Startup Recovery Assistant; macOS restored via "Disco di avvio" (F-41). Marco and Ciani were rightly upset: the count is now a hard rule.

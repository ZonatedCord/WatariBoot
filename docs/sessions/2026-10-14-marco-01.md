## What it means (Opus's reading, to be confirmed)
- **V2:** without a GICD in the MADT Windows does not reach the session: it is the first experimental sign that today's HAL demands a "real" GIC. Consistent with F-04 and F-05. It is negative for the clean native route.
- **V3 is half-good news:** today's Windows ARM64 **still uses the parking protocol** (this was not guaranteed: it was G4's risk no. 1), so starting the cores without PSCI is a real route. The 0x5C bugcheck comes **after** the core's jump: most likely it is a problem of ours in the handshake or in the core state (for example an expected wake-up IPI, the state of the registers or of the per-CPU GIC), hence solvable.
- Overall: the secondary cores look solvable; the GIC remains the main obstacle (V2), so Plan B (an EL2 layer presenting the GIC) remains the most likely. Marco decides.

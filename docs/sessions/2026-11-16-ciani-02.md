## What it means
1. On the M1 the layer cannot catch the PMU at EL2: the MDCR_EL2 PMU trap bits do not exist without PMUv3. Catch mode for the PMU is a dead end on this CPU; only the EDK2 EL1 net sees PMU accesses.
2. bootmgr's PMU use does not depend on `ID_AA64DFR0_EL1.PMUVer`; hiding the PMU does not change the path.
3. [hypothesis] After the first PMCCNTR read nothing reaches EDK2's vectors any more (no `PMCR_EL0` EL1EMU line, although the bench order is PMCCNTR then PMCR). Candidate: from there bootmgr runs with its own exception vectors, and the next UNDEFINED goes to its own handler. Not checked: we do not analyse Microsoft binaries (F-01).
